# Shorts Studio Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

## What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Shorts Studio uses Google sign-in to access the owner's own YouTube channels (uploading videos and reading channel analytics) and the owner's own Google Drive files used for those videos.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner their own statistics. It is stored only on the owner's own devices. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Shorts Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Contact

Questions can be raised at https://github.com/spikle/shorts-studio/issues.
